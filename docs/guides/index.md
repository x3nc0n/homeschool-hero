# User Guides

Homeschool Hero serves three kinds of users, each with a tailored experience. Find your role below to jump straight to the right guide.

---

## Who are you?

### 👩‍👧 Parent or Teacher

You are the primary manager of the homeschool workspace. You set up students and subjects, plan lessons, create assignments, upload student work, grade it, and generate reports. Everything in the app flows through you.

**→ [Parent & Teacher Guide](./parents)**

---

### 🎒 Student

You use Homeschool Hero to stay organized. You can see today's schedule, check what assignments are coming up, view your grades, upload finished work, and track your progress over time.

**→ [Student Guide](./students)**

---

### 🔧 Administrator

Administrators manage the platform itself — users, family accounts, RBAC roles, system health, and compliance configuration. If you are running a self-hosted install or supporting multiple families, start with the Admin Guide.

**→ [Administrator Guide](../admin-guide)**

## Role reference

| Role | What they can do |
|---|---|
| **Admin** | Everything — platform management, all families, all data |
| **Parent / Teacher** | Full household: students, curriculum, assignments, grading, reports |
| **Student** | Read-only views scoped to their own data; can upload work |

> **Note:** Parent and Teacher share the same permissions level in Homeschool Hero. A tutor added to your family gets the same teaching and grading access.
