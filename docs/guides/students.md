# Student Guide

Welcome to **Homeschool Hero**! 🎉

This guide is for you — the student. Homeschool Hero keeps your school life organized in one place: your schedule, your assignments, your grades, and your work. This page explains what you can see and do.

---

## Signing In

Your parent or teacher will set up your account and send you an invitation link.

### First-time sign-in

1. Open the invitation link.
2. Enter your email address.
3. Choose your display name.
4. Create a password.
5. Finish setup — you will land on your personal dashboard.

### Every time after that

1. Open the Homeschool Hero sign-in page.
2. Enter your email and password (or use the sign-in method your family uses).
3. Select **Sign in**.

::: tip Forgot your password?
Ask a parent or teacher to reset it for you. Do not guess too many times, as the account may lock.
:::

---

## Your Dashboard {#your-dashboard}

When you sign in, the dashboard gives you a quick picture of your school day.

**What you will see:**

| Widget | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Today's schedule | Your lessons and activities for today |
| Upcoming assignments | Everything due in the next 7 days |
| Recent grades | Your latest scored work |
| Attendance today | Whether you are marked present |
| Pacing alerts | If you are falling behind on lesson plans |
| Compliance notices | If any required records need attention |

The dashboard is designed to answer three questions:
1. **What do I need to do today?**
2. **What is due soon?**
3. **How am I doing?**

Use the **Refresh** button if anything looks out of date.

## Viewing Assignments {#viewing-assignments}

Open **Assignments** from the menu to see your full work list.

### Finding assignments

- **Search** by keyword (e.g., "Chapter 4 reading")
- **Filter** by subject, status, or due date
- **Sort** by due date to see the most urgent work first

### Assignment statuses

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Assigned** | Work is waiting for you |
| **Submitted** | Work has been turned in |
| **Graded** | Your work has been scored |
| **Excused** | You do not need to complete this one |

::: tip
Check the dashboard first for anything urgent, then open Assignments for the full picture.
:::

## Uploading Your Work {#uploading-work}

Homeschool Hero is built around the idea that students do their work **on paper** and then submit a photo or scan.

### How it works

1. Complete your assignment on paper.
2. Your parent or teacher will photograph or scan it.
3. They upload it from the **Uploads** page.
4. The system processes the file and grades it (automatically or with a parent review).
5. Your grade appears in the gradebook.

### You can also upload yourself

If your parent has given you access to the Uploads page, the process is:

1. Go to **Assignments & Grading → Uploads**.
2. Choose the **assignment** you completed.
3. Add your file — drag it in, browse for it, or tap **Use camera** on your phone.
4. Check the preview to make sure the photo is clear and all of the page is visible.
5. Tap **Upload submission**.

**Supported file types:** PDF, JPEG/JPG, PNG, HEIC/HEIF, TIFF, WEBP — up to 25 MB per file.

::: tip Good upload habits
- Lay the paper flat on a well-lit surface before photographing.
- Make sure all four corners of the page are in frame.
- If the photo is blurry, retake it — a clear photo means faster, more accurate grading.
:::

### Resubmitting

If you need to submit a better copy, you can upload again. The system keeps all versions but uses the newest one for grading.

## Checking Your Grades {#checking-your-grades}

Open **Grade Book** from the menu.

### What you can see

- **Overall GPA** — your combined score across all subjects
- **Subject averages** — your percentage and letter grade in each subject
- **Category breakdowns** — how you are doing in Homework vs. Quiz vs. Test separately
- **Trend charts** — whether your scores are going up or down over time
- **Grading period filter** — switch between fall semester, spring, etc.

::: tip
A single low score does not define your grade. Use it as a clue for what to practice next.
:::

## Tracking Your Progress

Homeschool Hero gives you several ways to see how you are growing.

### Planner

Go to **Academics → Planner** to review your daily and weekly schedule. You can see your lesson blocks and spot what is coming up each day.

### Lesson Plans

Go to **Academics → Lesson Plans** to see:
- The lessons planned for you
- The status of each lesson (planned, in progress, complete)
- Whether you are on pace for the year

### Report Cards

Go to **Reports → Report Cards** to review your grading-period summary. When a parent or teacher has finalized a report card, you can view it and download the PDF.

### Transcripts

Go to **Reports → Transcripts** for your full academic record: every course, grade, credit, and cumulative GPA. Useful for applications, evaluations, or just tracking your progress over years.

### Compliance

Go to **Reports → Compliance** to see whether required records (attendance, assessments, etc.) are on track for your state's requirements. This is mostly your parent's job, but it is good to know it exists.

## Your Portfolio {#portfolio}

Go to **Reports → Portfolio**.

Your portfolio is a personal collection of your best work and learning highlights.

### Adding to your portfolio

| Entry type | Good for |
|---|---|
| Work sample | A finished assignment or project you are proud of |
| Journal | A written reflection on what you learned |
| Milestone | A skill you mastered or a goal you reached |
| Photo | A hands-on project, experiment, or field trip |
| Note | A quick observation or thought |

You can attach files to any entry and link entries to subjects or specific assignments.

### Collections

Group entries into themed collections — for example:
- "Science fair project — spring 2026"
- "Reading growth samples"
- "Best work — year end"

A parent can mark a collection as **public**, which creates a link you can share with evaluators, grandparents, or co-ops.

## Search and Notifications

### Search

Use the search bar at the top of the page to find:
- Assignments
- Grades
- Notifications
- Portfolio entries and notes

Your account only searches information that belongs to you.

### Notifications

Open **Notifications** (the bell icon) to see:
- Upcoming due-date reminders
- Grading updates
- Invitation or account messages

Mark items as read once you have seen them so your notifications stay tidy.

## Appearance Settings

You can personalize your own view:

1. Go to **Settings → Appearance**.
2. Choose **Light**, **Dark**, or **High-contrast** theme.
3. Adjust accent color, font size, and display density if offered.
4. Changes save automatically.

---

## Getting Help

If something does not look right:

1. Refresh the page.
2. Check your dashboard and notifications for any alerts.
3. Make sure you are signed into the correct account.
4. Ask your parent or teacher — that is what they are there for.

**Ask for help when:**
- An assignment you finished is not showing up
- A grade looks wrong
- You cannot sign in
- You think a photo still needs to be uploaded

---

You have got this. One assignment at a time, one day at a time — Homeschool Hero is here to help you stay organized and see how much you are learning. 🌟
