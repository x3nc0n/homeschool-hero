# Student User Guide

## Welcome!

Hi! Welcome to **Homeschool Hero** 🎉  
This app helps you keep track of school in one place. You can use it to:

- see what is coming up
- check your schedule
- read lesson plans
- view assignments and grades
- follow your progress over time
- save your work in a portfolio

If your family or teacher set up a **student viewer** account for you, the app will only show **your** information.

---

## Logging In

Your parent, co-parent, or tutor will usually set up your account first and send you an invitation.

You may sign in in one of these ways:

- **Email + password**
- **School or family sign-in provider** if your setup uses OIDC or SAML

### First-time login

1. Open the invitation link you were given.
2. Enter your email address.
3. Choose your display name.
4. Create your password.
5. Finish setup and go to your dashboard.

### Later logins

1. Open the Homeschool Hero sign-in page.
2. Enter your email and password, or use the sign-in button your family uses.
3. Select **Sign in**.

**Tip:** If your password does not work, ask a parent or teacher to help instead of guessing too many times.

---

## Your Dashboard

When you log in, your dashboard gives you a quick look at your school day.

You may see:

- **Today’s schedule**
- **Upcoming assignments** due in the next 7 days
- **Recent grades**
- **Attendance today**
- **Pacing alerts** if you are falling behind
- **Compliance warnings** if something important still needs attention

Your dashboard is meant to answer three big questions:

1. What do I need to do today?
2. What is due soon?
3. How am I doing so far?

Use the **Refresh** button if something looks out of date.

---

## Viewing Assignments

Open **Assignments** from the menu to see your work list.

There you can:

- look through assigned work
- search by keyword
- filter by subject, student, status, or due date
- sort assignments by what is due first

Assignments can show:

- the **title**
- the **subject**
- the **due date**
- the current **status**

Common assignment statuses:

- **Assigned** – work is waiting for you
- **Submitted** – work was turned in
- **Graded** – the work has been scored
- **Excused** – you do not need to complete it

**Tip:** Check the dashboard first for urgent work, then open Assignments for the full list.

## Submitting Work

Homeschool Hero has a full upload and grading system, but in the current app setup the **Uploads** page is available to parents, co-parents, and tutors.

### What this means for students right now

- You can **see assignment progress**
- You can **see grades after work is scored**
- A parent or tutor may need to **upload the finished assignment for you**

### What you can do yourself

You can still save learning evidence in **Portfolio**:

- add a **work sample**
- write a **journal** entry
- save a **milestone**
- upload a **photo**
- attach files to portfolio entries

This is helpful for projects, essays, pictures of hands-on work, or a learning journal.

**Good plan:** Finish your assignment, then:

1. ask your parent or tutor to upload it if needed
2. add it to your portfolio if you want to keep a record of your work

---

## Checking Your Grades

Open **Grade Book** to see your results.

You can:

- view grades by student, subject, and grading period
- see your **overall GPA**
- view your percentage in each subject
- check letter grades when they are available
- look at trend charts to see how your scores change over time

You may also see **Recent grades** on your dashboard.

**Tip:** One low score does not define you. Use grades as clues for what to practice next.

## Tracking Your Progress

Homeschool Hero gives you a few ways to see growth over time.

### Planner

Use **Planner** to review your daily or weekly schedule and agenda.

### Lesson Plans

Use **Lesson Plans** to see:

- planned lessons
- lesson status
- pacing information

### Report Cards

Use **Report Cards** to review grading-period summaries. You may be able to open a PDF copy when one has been created.

### Transcripts

Use **Transcripts** to view long-term academic records, including GPA and course history, when your family has generated them.

### Compliance

Use **Compliance** to see whether required school records are on track for your state.

### Portfolio

Use **Portfolio** to build a record of your learning with:

- work samples
- journal entries
- milestones
- photos
- shared collections

Portfolio is a great place to keep work you are proud of.

## Search, Notifications, and Helpful Extras

### Search

The search bar at the top can help you find:

- assignments
- grades
- notifications
- notes and other school items

Your account only searches information you are allowed to see.

### Notifications

Open **Notifications** to keep up with:

- due dates
- grading updates
- reminders
- invitation and account messages

You can mark items as read when you are done with them.

### Appearance and settings

You can also adjust your own:

- appearance
- notification preferences

---

## AI and Grading

Homeschool Hero supports AI-assisted grading in some family setups. This can help adults score work faster.

Right now, there is **no student chat or tutoring tool built into the app**. If AI grading is turned on, you may simply notice that feedback appears faster after an upload is reviewed.

---

## Getting Help

If something is not working:

1. refresh the page
2. check your dashboard and notifications
3. make sure you are signed into the correct account
4. ask your parent, co-parent, or tutor for help

Ask for help if:

- an assignment is missing
- the wrong grade appears
- you cannot log in
- you think a file still needs to be uploaded
